# Critical Thinking

Challenge the work constructively. The goal is a better decision, not endless skepticism.

## Review Lens

Ask:

- What evidence supports each important claim?
- Which assumptions are untested or hidden?
- Could any fact, citation, calculation, or capability be hallucinated?
- Is there a simpler solution, including no AI?
- What counterexample would overturn the conclusion?
- Which edge cases, incentives, or distribution shifts could break it?
- What is the cost of false positives, false negatives, and failure?

## Workflow

1. Reconstruct the claim and intended decision fairly.
2. Separate facts, inferences, assumptions, and preferences.
3. Test the strongest plausible alternative, not a weak straw man.
4. Rank issues by likelihood and impact.
5. Verify high-impact uncertainties or propose a concrete test.
6. Conclude with keep, revise, test, or reject.

Do not block progress over low-impact uncertainty. State confidence and what new evidence would change the recommendation.

## Output

Lead with the verdict. Then list decisive evidence, critical assumptions, top failure modes, strongest alternative, and the next validation action.
