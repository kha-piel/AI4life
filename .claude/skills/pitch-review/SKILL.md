# Pitch Review

Make the value, evidence, differentiation, and feasibility easy to understand under time pressure.

## Workflow

1. Identify audience, judging criteria, time limit, desired action, and likely objections.
2. Test the narrative: user and pain, why now, solution, core demo, evidence, differentiation, feasibility, impact, and ask.
3. Remove claims that lack evidence or qualify them precisely. Prefer measured outcomes and concrete user stories over technical name-dropping.
4. Ensure the demo proves the central value with a short happy path and visible result. Prepare seeded inputs, backup recording or screenshots, and recovery language.
5. Explain AI choices in terms of necessity, reliability, evaluation, safety, cost, and limitations.
6. Rehearse timing and adversarial questions. Put detail in backup material rather than overloading the main story.

## Output

Return:

- one-sentence value proposition;
- recommended story order and cuts;
- claim-to-evidence gaps;
- demo script with fallback;
- likely judge questions with concise answers;
- final verdict against the judging criteria.

The pitch passes when a listener can retell the problem, target user, unique value, proof, and next step without seeing the slides.
